01 — Service

Shadow Studies

We specialize in quantitative shadow impact assessments, Section 295 compliance, shadow envelope studies, and location-duration heatmapping for CEQA and planning review.

Our shadow consulting practice is built on over a decade of experience navigating California's most demanding regulatory requirements, from San Francisco to Solano County and beyond.

02 — Service

Visual Studies

Our visual simulation and photomontage services create compelling, accurate visualizations that communicate complex design proposals to stakeholders, planning commissions, and communities.

Each simulation is built on precise 3D modeling, carefully matched site photography, and meticulous photo compositing to produce photorealistic representations that facilitate informed decision-making.

03 — Service

Daylight Studies

Our daylight analysis services evaluate natural light access conditions for proposed developments and their surrounding environments. Using advanced 3D modeling and simulation tools, we quantify how new construction will affect daylight availability.

These analyses provide the technical foundation for informed decisions about building massing, setbacks, and design modifications that balance development goals with daylight preservation.
