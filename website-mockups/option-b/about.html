About the Firm

PreVision Design stands at the forefront of specialized architectural consulting, delivering precise technical analysis and visual simulation services that bridge the critical intersection of design, regulation, and environmental stewardship. Based in Marin County, our boutique firm has established itself as the premier resource for complex shadow analysis, CEQA compliance, and visual impact assessment throughout California.

The breadth of our project portfolio demonstrates our versatility and technical depth. We have collaborated with leading environmental consulting firms including ESA, ICF, SWCA, and Stantec, as well as prominent developers like Tishman Speyer, Related California, and TMG Partners. Our geographic reach extends throughout the Bay Area and California, with projects spanning from high-density urban developments in San Francisco's Mission Bay and SOMA districts to suburban contexts in Redwood City and Emeryville.

Our technical capabilities set us apart in the industry. We have developed proprietary software tools that enable rapid, accurate modeling of complex shadow phenomena. Our workflow integrates advanced 3D modeling, GIS analysis, field observation, and custom visualization techniques to deliver comprehensive technical documentation that meets the most demanding regulatory requirements.
